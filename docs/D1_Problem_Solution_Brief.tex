\section{The Problem}

\begin{mdframed}[backgroundcolor=tracered!7,linecolor=tracered,linewidth=0.7pt,innerleftmargin=7pt,innerrightmargin=7pt,innertopmargin=4pt,innerbottommargin=4pt]
\small Indian banks lost \textbf{Rs.~71,543~Cr} to fraud in FY~2024-25 (RBI Annual Report). The infrastructure designed to prevent this is not underfunded; it is broken by design.
\end{mdframed}

\subsection{Who Is Affected and How Severely}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Direct:} Union Bank's AML compliance teams process hundreds of false alerts daily. Genuine fraud rings are buried due to investigator fatigue.
  \item \textbf{Financial:} IBM's 2024 Financial Services benchmark finds a \textbf{277-day median gap} between fraud initiation and detection. By then, recovery is effectively impossible.
  \item \textbf{Operational:} Manual Suspicious Transaction Report (STR) preparation takes \textbf{4 to 6 hours per case}. STR quality varies across officers, reducing the intelligence value of every FIU-IND filing.
  \item \textbf{Regulatory:} Under PMLA 2002, PSBs face penalties for late or incomplete STR filings.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Why Current Approaches Fail}

Rule-based AML systems at Indian PSBs operate on fixed thresholds, generating 95\%+ false positive rates. Three fundamental failures result:

\smallskip
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{L{3.0cm} X}
\toprule
\textbf{\color{traceblue}Failure Mode} & \textbf{Consequence} \\
\midrule
95\%+ false positives & Investigators spend their day dismissing noise. Genuine fraud rings are buried. \\[2pt]
Single-transaction scope & Multi-hop laundering is invisible to per-transaction rules. Funds move through 4 to 8 intermediate accounts with no single hop triggering a threshold. \\[2pt]
Reactive, not adaptive & Rules are static. New fraud typologies require a deployment cycle before becoming detectable. \\[2pt]
No cross-account correlation & Siloed systems cannot connect related accounts executing coordinated round-trips. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\subsection{The Patterns That Rules Miss}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Layering chains:} Funds traverse 4 to 8 intermediate accounts, each forwarding more than 70\% of received value within 48 hours. No single hop is suspicious; the chain is.
  \item \textbf{Round-trip (circular flow):} Money exits Account A, passes through 3 to 6 accounts, and returns within 72 hours. Net movement is zero. The purpose is to fabricate a paper trail of legitimate activity.
  \item \textbf{Structuring:} Multiple transactions just below Rs.~10~lakh are split across beneficiaries on the same day to evade CTR thresholds.
\end{itemize}

\section{The Solution: TRACE.ai}

\textbf{TRACE.ai} is a graph-native AML intelligence platform. Every banking transaction becomes a directed edge in a dynamic graph. Fraud patterns emerge as structural properties of the graph, detectable only at the network level.

\subsection{Core Architecture: Four Parallel Detection Signals}

\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{L{2.8cm} X}
\toprule
\textbf{\color{traceblue}Signal (weight)} & \textbf{How It Works} \\
\midrule
Pattern Matcher (30\%) & Five AML typology detectors on a NetworkX MultiDiGraph: circular flow, layering, structuring, mule fan-in/out, dormant burst. \\[2pt]
Graph Classifier (30\%) & XGBoost on 11 graph-structural features (PageRank, betweenness, velocity, KYC risk). AUC above 0.99 on synthetic test data. \\[2pt]
Online Anomaly (20\%) & River HalfSpaceTrees per-account behavioral baselines with ADWIN drift detection, sub-millisecond latency, no batch retraining. \\[2pt]
Compliance Engine (20\%) & YAML-defined rules encoding RBI Master Directions, PMLA 2002, and FIU-IND STR triggers. Hot-reloadable without a system restart. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\smallskip
Fused thresholds: \texttt{CRITICAL} $\geq 0.85$, \texttt{HIGH} $\geq 0.70$, \texttt{MEDIUM} $\geq 0.50$.

\subsection{Intelligence Layer}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{SHAP Explainability:} TreeExplainer attribution on every alert, showing the top-5 graph features that drove the risk score.
  \item \textbf{Gemini LLM Explainer:} Structured evidence sent to Gemini 2.5 Flash for a 2 to 3 sentence plain-English explanation per alert.
  \item \textbf{NL Investigation Copilot:} Officers ask in English (e.g., ``Show circular flows above Rs.~5~lakh in the last 7~days''); the system dispatches to 6 pattern-specific graph query handlers.
  \item \textbf{Auto-STR Generator:} One click produces a FIU-IND compliant 8-section A4 PDF. Generation takes under 5 minutes (previously 4 to 6 hours per case).
\end{itemize}
